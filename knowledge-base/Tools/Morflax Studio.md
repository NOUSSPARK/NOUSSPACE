# Morflax Studio

**URL:** https://studio.morflax.com/
**Type:** Tool — browser-based 3D design & motion platform

## What it is

Morflax Studio is a web-based 3D design and motion toolset. It offers abstract/shape generators, vector-to-3D conversion, device/clothing/branding mockups, icon editor, scene builder, effects & post-processing, and template remixing — all in the browser.

## Why it matters for Nous Space ⭐

- **Highlighted** — perfectly aligned with the Nous Space vision: 3D, motion, mockups, and cinematic visuals in one browser tool.
- Great for producing brand mockups, device frames, 3D icons, and post-processed imagery for the site and blog.
- Complements Three.js (code) and zozuar (procedural shaders) with a no-code/low-code 3D creation path.

## Key capabilities

- Abstract & shape 3D generators
- Vector (.svg) → 3D
- Device, clothing, branding mockups
- Icon & illustration editor
- Scene builder (combine elements)
- Effects & post-processing (cinematic filters)
- Template catalog + remix
