## What it is

"Tortoise" is a free, open-source generator for **toroidal fractal artwork**. It renders recursive Hopf tori with chirality fibers, nested shells, particles, and gold shading, then can send the rendered view as a structure reference to Stability AI to repaint it from a prompt.

- Combines parametric geometry with AI image generation — bridges code-driven (zozuar) and AI-driven (Odyssey) aesthetics.

## Key capabilities

- Fractal torus structure (fibers, chirality, recursive levels, detuning, nesting)
- Phenomenology presets (Vishvarupa, Ophanim, Kundalini, etc.)
- Gold shading, bloom, vignette, depth of field
- Animation (counter-rotation, particle flow)
- Export presets (JSON)
- Stability AI image generation from the rendered structure
- Open source
