# Nous Space — Visual Direction

A working definition of the Nous Space visual language, built from highlighted references.

## Core themes

- **Cosmic & organic unity** — nature, the human body, and the universe are aligned (eye ≈ galaxy). Inner space mirrors outer space.
- **Motion-first** — motion carries meaning; minimal copy, striking single clips.
- **Code-driven** — procedural shaders and 3D (Three.js) as first-class visuals.
- **Dark, cinematic, atmospheric** — moody lighting, minimal palette, strong typography.

## Influences

- ⭐ **Odyssey** (X) — dark, cinematic, atmospheric video direction; "Could be a long night.", "Still somewhere in the pattern." Used for branding.
- ⭐ **zozuar** (X) — procedural GLSL "tweet shaders": raymarched, organic, psychedelic, volumetric noise. Perfectly aligned with the Nous Space vision.
- **Vessa** — animated live brand guidelines; click-to-copy values; motion as shipped numbers.
- **Three.js** — 3D scenes, heroes, interactive accents.
- **Poincaré sphere {3,5,3}** — wireframe mathematical 3D visuals (cosmic geometry).
- **Nature/Body/Universe** — eye-as-galaxy; cosmic + organic unity.

## Palette (starting point)

- Ink `#101216`, Paper `#EEF0F3` (Vessa-inspired), with accent blue `#1F2DE6`.
- Dark-first, with light mode.

## Motion principles

- Short looping clips as heroes.
- Procedural shaders for ambient backgrounds.
- Copyable motion values (easing/duration) for developers.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Post-processing palette (from Morflax Effects)

Lens Distortion, Fractal Glass, Glitch, Dither, ASCII, Glass Lens, CRT, Bricks, Dot Matrix, Outline, CMYK, Risograph, Mosaic, Heatmap, Gradient Map — plus Blur, Bloom, Vignette, Chromatic, Film Grain, Gradient Overlay. See [[Morflax Effects]].
