# Nous Space — Visual Taste & Direction

A living, detailed definition of the Nous Space visual taste, compiled from every reference, tool, and idea gathered so far.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy

Nous Space is **cosmic, contemplative, and code-driven**. The name "Nous" (mind/intelligence) anchors everything: the site is a **microcosm** — a small universe that holds galaxies of ideas. Every mind is a microcosm; every work is a star.

Three unifying beliefs:

1. **Inner space mirrors outer space** — nature, the human body, and the universe are aligned (an eye ≈ a galaxy).
2. **Motion carries meaning** — minimal copy, striking visuals; motion is first-class content.
3. **Code is the medium** — procedural shaders, math, and 3D are not decoration; they *are* the brand.

---

## 2. Mood & Atmosphere

- Dark-first, cinematic, atmospheric, moody.
- Contemplative, quiet, "the eternal silence of these infinite spaces."
- Optimistic yet profound ("the inevitable splendor was there in the beginning").
- Sci-fi classical — statues, sacred geometry, cosmic structures.
- Organic + digital: decay, growth, erosion, loops, particles.

Keywords: **cosmic, minimal, hypnotic, procedural, organic, sacred geometry, microcosm, galaxies of ideas, code-as-art, motion-first.**

## 3. Color Palette

Starting palette (inspired by Vessa + cosmic references):

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#101216` | Primary dark background |
| Paper | `#EEF0F3` | Light surface / text on dark |
| Accent Blue | `#1F2DE6` | CTAs, links, highlights |
| Zinc scale | Tailwind defaults | Neutrals, borders, muted text |

- Dark mode is the default; light mode supported.
- High contrast, minimal color. Color is used sparingly and meaningfully (like Vessa's click-to-copy swatches).
- Gold/amber reserved for special moments (Tortoise gold shading).

---

## 4. Typography

- System font stack initially (offline build); Geist can be reintroduced when network is stable.
- Monospace (`font-mono`) for code, values, technical details.
- Large, tight tracking for headlines (`tracking-tight`); relaxed leading for body.
- Minimal type scale; let whitespace and motion carry hierarchy.

---

## 5. Motion Principles

1. **Short looping clips as heroes** — a single striking clip carries the message (Odyssey).
2. **Procedural shaders for ambient backgrounds** — zozuar-style GLSL, audio-reactive.
3. **Copyable motion values** — easing/duration as real numbers, like Vessa (CSS/GSAP-ready).
4. **Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** — always provide a static fallback.
5. **Seamless loops** — no visible seams; hypnotic, meditative (A.L. Crego).
6. **Audio-reactive** where appropriate — sound experiments (energy/frequency/vibration).

## 13. Design System Principles

- Minimal palette, high contrast.
- Monospace for code/values; sans for prose.
- Whitespace + motion over decoration.
- Components via shadcn/ObsidianUI; own the code.
- Respect reduced motion; provide static fallbacks.
- Dark-first with light mode.

---

## 14. What to Avoid

- Generic AI-aesthetic (hyper-smooth, no substance).
- Over-decoration; clutter.
- Heavy color; keep it restrained.
- Motion without meaning.
