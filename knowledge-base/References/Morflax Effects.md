# Morflax Studio — Effects (Visual Directions)

Source: https://studio.morflax.com/effects

A catalog of stylized filters, cinematic post-processing, and final image adjustments available in Morflax Studio. Each can be applied to an uploaded image or video.

## Effects

1. **Lens Distortion** — warps/curves the frame (New)
2. **Fractal Glass** — fractal-style glass refraction (New)
3. **Glitch** — digital glitch / datamosh artifacts (New)
4. **Dither** — ordered dithering / retro pixel quantization
5. **ASCII** — image converted to ASCII characters
6. **Glass Lens** — glass/lens refraction look (New)
7. **CRT** — cathode-ray tube scanlines & phosphor (New)
8. **Bricks** — brick/tiled mosaic patterning
9. **Dot Matrix** — halftone dot-matrix rendering
10. **Outline** — edge/outline extraction
11. **CMYK** — CMYK color separation / print look
12. **Risograph** — risograph print texture & color
13. **Mosaic** — tiled mosaic abstraction
14. **Heatmap** — thermal/heatmap color mapping
15. **Gradient Map** — remap tones to a gradient (New)

## Editor-only adjustments

- Blur, Bloom, Vignette, Chromatic (aberration), Film Grain, Gradient Overlay

## Relevance to Nous Space

- Strong palette of **post-processing directions** for hero imagery, blog visuals, and brand mockups.
- Pairs with the cosmic/organic + cinematic visual direction (Odyssey, zozuar).
- Risograph/CMYK/Dither/ASCII give a tactile, print-like texture option; Glitch/CRT give digital edge; Heatmap/Gradient Map give data-art feel.
