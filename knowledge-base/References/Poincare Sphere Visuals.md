## What it is

A Blender render of a **Poincaré sphere {3,5,3}** (Poincaré dodecahedral space) as a **wireframe only, 4 layers**. The {3,5,3} Schläfli symbol describes a regular 4D polytope (120-cell dual) projected into 3D — a deeply symmetric, nested spherical lattice.
