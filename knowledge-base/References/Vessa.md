## Why it stands out ⭐

- Animated, live brand guidelines delivered as a link (not a PDF).
- Click-to-copy colors, motion values shown as real numbers (easing/duration).
- AI-assisted: Claude/Cursor can build the whole guideline via MCP.
- Clean, minimal, high-contrast design (Ink `#101216` / Paper `#EEF0F3`).
- One-time pricing (€39/brand) — no subscription.

## Key ideas to borrow

- Motion as first-class content with copyable values.
- "Live page" instead of static document.
- Click-to-copy interaction pattern.
- Minimal palette + strong typography.
