# zozuar (@zozuar) — Yonatan

**Profile:** https://x.com/zozuar
**Type:** Reference / Inspiration — procedural / shader art

## What his visuals are

Yonatan ("zozuar") is a shader artist who creates **"tweet shaders"**: complete, animated visual pieces written in **GLSL in under 280 characters**, run through [twigl.app](https://twigl.app).

His visuals are:

- **Procedural & mathematical** — generated entirely from code, no textures or models.
- **Volumetric / raymarched** — foggy tunnels, clouds, organic structures built by marching rays through noise fields.
- **Organic & psychedelic** — jellyfish, flowers, neuro-noise, swirling patterns that feel alive.
- **Minimal yet dense** — a handful of lines of math produce rich, evolving imagery.
- **Animated over time** — time variable drives motion; forms breathe, swirl, and morph.

## Style keywords

procedural, GLSL, raymarching, fractal noise (FBM), volumetric, organic, psychedelic, minimal code, tweet shader, twigl.
